01 — Who We Are

A Kenyan waste management and material recovery company.

Westport Industrial City collects, sorts and recovers waste and surplus material from institutions across Kenya — turning what's no longer needed into resale value or safe, compliant disposal.

02 — What We Do

Collection, recovery, and responsible disposal.

We handle paper, metal, plastic, furniture and e-waste — sorting each stream for resale where it has value, and for safe disposal where it doesn't. Every engagement is documented, so our clients keep a clean paper trail.

Paper Metal Plastic Furniture E-Waste Other Materials

03 — How We Do It

A straightforward process, done properly.

We visit, assess what's there, and agree what should be sold and what should be disposed of. Materials are sorted and categorised on site, then moved to sale or licensed disposal — with sign-off at every step.

04 — What Sets Us Apart

Circular by design, transparent by default.

We look for the highest-value use of a material before it's scrapped, keep clients informed at every stage, and operate within Kenya's public procurement and environmental compliance requirements.
