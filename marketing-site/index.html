What we do

One operating layer for public-sector material recovery.

Institutional mapping

Every ministry, state department and public institution registered against the official government hierarchy — 376 entities and counting.

Relationship Manager network

A dedicated field team engages each institution directly, logging activity and building the relationship that makes recovery possible.

Categorised recovery

Material streams are sorted for sale or disposal across defined categories — paper, metal, plastic, e-waste and furniture.

Transparent reporting

Every engagement, submission and facilitation request is logged and auditable — from field visit to recovered value.

How it works

From institution to recovered material, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Map the institution

    Every ministry, state corporation, county government and public body is registered against Kenya's official government structure — so nothing sits outside the network.

  2. 02

    Assign a Relationship Manager

    Institutions are matched to a Relationship Manager who owns that relationship end to end — the single point of contact in the field.

  3. 03

    Engage and log material streams

    Visits, calls and follow-ups are logged against each institution, and recoverable material is categorised for sale or disposal.

  4. 04

    Track recovery and reporting

    Every submission rolls up into a live register — by ministry, by county, by material — so progress is always visible, not just promised.
