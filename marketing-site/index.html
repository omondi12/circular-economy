Circular Recovery

Every asset earns its highest value before anything is scrapped.

1 Redeploy or transfer Another public entity needs it
2 Sell as a working asset Highest value, widest market
3 Refurbish and sell Low-cost repair lifts the price
4 Sell for parts Engines, motors, boards, components
5 Recycle as commodity Weighed steel, copper, aluminium, tyres
6 Scrap Only once every step above is exhausted
7 Regulated destruction Licensed, certified — via Lot 2

Scrap is the last value state — never the first valuation.

The Consortium

One partnership delivers the whole disposal journey.

Framework & programme

HOP Global and AMAC, Lot 1 partners: entity liaison, register intake, lotting and reporting.

Auction execution & global reach

Circular recovery

Licensed Lot 2 handling of e-waste, medical, industrial and other residue.

The disposal decision stays with the public entity. Its Accounting Officer and Disposal Committee decide; the consortium executes the approved disposal.
