# Westport Industrial City — Circular Economy Platform: Design Direction

## Confirmed Direction
- **Identity & Essence**: Kenya's public-sector circular economy operating layer, bridging 668+ institutions across 47 counties and 24 ministries.
- **Mood**: Cinematic, tactile, institutional yet strikingly modern, high-precision industrial ecology, confident, credible, and energetic without feeling frivolous.
- **Color Philosophy**:
  - Deep Forest & Emerald Greens (`#0a3722`, `#0f5c37`, `#147041`, `#1f8c52`) reflecting regenerative systems and environmental authority.
  - Warm Earth Golds (`#b5810a`, `#e3ad3f`, `#966b08`) signifying recovered institutional value, material dignity, and public trust.
  - Canvas & Clean Off-White (`#f6faf6`, `#ffffff`, `#f0f6f1`) for airy readability, crisp architectural spacing, and institutional clarity.
  - Charcoal Inks (`#16211a`, `#5b6b60`, `#8b9a90`) for precise typography and high-contrast accessibility.
- **Typography**:
  - Display: `Instrument Serif` (editorial, authoritative, historic gravitas, dynamic italics).
  - Body / UI: `Instrument Sans` (ergonomic, clean, contemporary, legible at dense metrics).
  - Eyebrows & Monospace Data: `IBM Plex Mono` (field logbook feel, registry IDs, counts, timestamps).
- **Signature Visual Elements**:
  - Live orbiting Circular Network Hero visual (dual counter-rotating arcs, glowing pulses, interactive node anchors).
  - Real-time animated number ticker with smooth eased incrementation.
  - Multi-tier material stream selector with live data badges and dynamic stream filtering.
  - Interactive Kenya coverage dotfield grid with reactive proximity ripples, ministry/county filter toggles, and live counter breakdowns.
  - Parallax depth cards, glassmorphic floating navigation with backdrop blur, and magnetic buttons with cursor-proximity physics.
  - Subtle architectural grid background overlay that feels like official institutional blueprints.
